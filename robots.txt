  Crawler policy for from.so. Everything is public, and the point of the file
  is to be explicit about it: an absent robots.txt also means "allowed", but
  it gives no place to say so, and no place to point at the sitemap.

  Liquid so the sitemap line follows site.url rather than hard-coding the host;
  permalink so the file lands at the root under the name crawlers look for.

  The AI crawlers are named rather than folded into `*` so that a later
  decision to keep any of them out is one line here, not a rewrite. Today each
  is allowed. The names are the user agents their operators document:
  Google-Extended is Google's opt-out for Gemini training (it does not affect
  Search), GPTBot OpenAI's, ClaudeBot Anthropic's, PerplexityBot and CCBot
  (Common Crawl) the other two that honour this file.
{%- endcomment -%}
