{%- comment -%} The light/dark/auto toggle. _includes/theme_script.html finds it by data-theme-toggle, gives it a real aria-label, and cycles it on click. Three states, so the icon shows the state the site is *in* rather than the one a click would move to. With two states "click me to go dark" was unambiguous; with three, an icon meaning "next" cannot say which of the other two is next, and a control that reports its own state is what a reader can actually check at a glance. The aria-label carries both: what is on now, and what the next click does. All three icons are in the markup and CSS shows whichever matches data-theme, rather than the script swapping a class. That way the right icon is there at first paint -- the theme is decided in , but the script's DOM work does not happen until DOMContentLoaded, and picking the icon in JS would show the wrong one until then. bi-circle-half for auto is the same glyph Bootstrap's own docs use for it, and it reads as "half one, half the other" without needing a legend. Parameters: class extra classes for the call site to skin the button with. The two layouts style it differently and load different stylesheets, so .theme-toggle carries only the behaviour the two share (hidden until the script has run, and which icon shows) and this carries the look. The site navbar passes Bootstrap's .nav-link; the CV passes .d-print-none, since a toggle means nothing on paper. {%- endcomment -%}
