Screen-only -- d-print-none keeps it out of the print/PDF CV, which reproduces the old LaTeX CV and has no place for hyperlinked chrome. cv.css renders it two ways from this one block of markup: on viewports wide enough to have real margin beside the paper it is a fixed rail that persists while the reader scrolls, and on everything narrower it stays here in the flow, a static block at the top of the page, above the name, that scrolls away with the page. The row/col wrapper is what aligns that in-flow variant with the main column; the rail variant flattens it. The header text links back to the top -- "#top" matches no element on the page, which is the HTML-spec'd way to say "scroll to the very top" -- and each section target id sits on the section element itself. The nested lists are the h3 subsections of their section, linked by the slugified group title, which is exactly the id cv_section_heading.html stamps on the h3 -- the two agree by construction. They render in the rail alone, unfolding under the section the reader is in: the reveal keys off the .active that the scroll-spy places, so with JavaScript off (or in the flow variant) they are simply never shown and the outline stays the flat list of sections. {% endcomment %}
